A language model you can read

Every middle neuron has a human-readable name. explain() returns the exact arithmetic behind a decision, not a story about it.

Built from scratch in NumPy: own tensors, forward pass, backward pass, losses, optimiser, KV cache. No PyTorch, no autograd library, no pretrained weights. Checkpoint: nodirect1024_wide60_lam1.0_data4m_e12_s0_best.npz. Model accuracy: 24.58% +/- 0.055 next-token accuracy, three seeds.

What this page does not prove. Concept names detect their concept in the INPUT (AUROC 0.9997) but their answer rows hold only 0.08% of their own words' probability. The explanation is exact; the names are not yet meaningful.

Why the explanation is trustworthy

The concept contributions plus the bias sum to the model's own logit, and the residual of that sum is reported next to every row. Concept share 1.000000 means the decision is carried entirely by the named concepts, with no hidden pathway.
