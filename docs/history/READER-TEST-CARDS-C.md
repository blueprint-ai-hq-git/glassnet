**The one metric in GlassNet judged by a human, not computed.**

so scoring its own test would be circular.

## What to do

Each card gives a concept's **name** and **10 windows in random order**. Mark **Y** if
you think that neuron fires on the window, **N** if not. **Exactly 5 of the 10 fire** —
that is the only hint, and it is a fair one.

**8 of 10 correct (0.80) or better = the name genuinely tells a reader something.**
Chance is 5/10. The answer key is `runs/t3/reader_test_key_c.json` — do not open it
until the cards are filled in.
