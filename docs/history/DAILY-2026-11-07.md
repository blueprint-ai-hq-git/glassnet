## Corrections published today (our own mistakes)
- `tokens per parameter` was 32x low in every log (it counted windows, not tokens); fix prints both ratios.
- "23.74 is the best d=256 number on record" was **false** - the full-corpus record is 35.72% by position
  (`STATE.md:146`). Corrected in four places.
- "The data lever bends flat" was a property of the method, not the data: ungated it kept climbing.
- Verifier blind spot: seed-0 200k arm had no marker and was silently skipped.
- Citation checker: 54 citations audited, 2 dead citations found in our own docs, fixed.
- Grader: three separate hardcoded-3 assumptions (epoch count, epoch-3 fallback, `/3` divisor) removed.

## Known limits stated, not hidden
- One seed on most ladder arms; the measured spread (3.07 pp) is larger than several deltas quoted.
- Above 200k train windows the trainer skips its exact train/val disjointness walk (disjoint by
  construction, corpus-level duplication never tested).
- `tokens/param` counts token instances, not distinct tokens (stride = window/2), so distinct coverage is
  about half of what the log prints.
- The 37.37% headline config has never been run in the configuration that now measures best.
