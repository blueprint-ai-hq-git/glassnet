# Licensing, in plain English

**Free for:** individuals, students, teachers, researchers, hobbyists. Use it, study it, change it, publish
what you make. No payment, no permission needed.

**Free for businesses** while the company's annual revenue is under **1 million USD**. Yes, real companies
can ship it while they are small. No paperwork.

**Above 1 million USD revenue:** you need a commercial licence from us. Our standard terms are a **1% revenue
share** on what the product earns using this code, or another fee we agree in writing.

**Not licensed at all:** harmful use (weapons, unlawful surveillance of people, content exploiting minors,
mass deception about whether a human or a machine is speaking).

**Why not plain MIT or Apache?** Because those give away commercial rights permanently, from the first
published version, and that cannot be reversed later. We wanted free for people and students, free for small
companies, and a paid deal at scale. That needs its own licence.

**Honest notes:**
- This licence is **not OSI-approved open source**. Some companies and Linux distributions will not use
  software under it. That is the trade-off for having a revenue model.
- We **cannot change this later for versions already released.** Once v1.0 is out under these terms, v1.0
  stays under these terms forever. Choose once, carefully.
- The licence text is a **draft** and must be reviewed by a lawyer before release. Nothing here is legal
  advice.
