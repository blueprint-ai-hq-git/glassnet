## Synthesis

### (a) What a credible community LM project actually needs
1. **Durable, non-grant-dependent compute** — EleutherAI leans on sponsors (CoreWeave/Stability/HF); BigScience leaned on French public HPC; Prime Intellect/Gensyn/Nous replace it with donated heterogeneous GPU. Nothing ships without it.
2. **Clean data licensing** — the credible projects converge on permissive, redistributable corpora: The Pile (MIT), Dolma / FineWeb / FineWeb-Edu / AmberDatasets (ODC-BY), RedPajama (Apache-2.0), The Stack (permissive-code + opt-out). Projects that can't redistribute their data (BLOOM ROOTS, Phi, OPT) fall back to "data cards" and lose reproducibility.
3. **A shared, decontaminated eval harness** — OLMo's **OLMES** + decontamination tooling, BabyLM's `babylm-eval`, nanochat/modded-nanogpt's fixed val-loss targets. Without this, "we trained a good model" is unfalsifiable.
4. **Governance & responsible-use** — BigScience ran open working groups and shipped a RAIL; Ai2 documents an ImpACT license. Community projects without governance get torn apart over data provenance.
5. **Dense release cadence (checkpoints/logs), not just final weights** — OLMo (all checkpoints + public W&B), LLM360 (360 checkpoints), Pythia (154 checkpoints) are the ones actually used for science.

### (b) Realistic bar for an individual in 2026
- **Train a GPT-2-capability LM from scratch: yes, cheaply.** nanochat: **~$48 (~2 h on 8×H100), ~$15 on spot**; llm.c: **~$20 for GPT-2 124M in 90 min**. This is a weekend-class project now.
- **Train a "real" small LM (1–3B): hundreds–thousands of $ and days.** Reference point: TinyLlama 1.1B / 3 T tokens ≈ 90 days on 16×A100. An individual can rent this, not own it.
- **Participate without training:** donate a GPU to Prime Intellect / Psyche / Petals / Gensyn (run a node), run third-party **audits** (Gensyn OPEN-1B), submit to BabyLM, or produce TinyStories/llm.c replications.
- **Contribute to a frontier open pretraining run:** realistically as data work, eval, or donated compute — not as a solo pretrainer.

### (c) Closest in spirit to "readable / interpretable model, community trains it"
There is an honest split — nobody has fully combined all three:
- **Interpretability-native:** **EleutherAI Pythia** is explicitly built "to facilitate interpretability research" (8 sizes sharing identical data order, 154 checkpoints each) — but it is not crowd-trained.
- **Auditability-native (the standout):** **Gensyn OPEN-1B / `open-transformers`** is the only project whose *selling point is auditability*: bitwise-reproducible training on commodity hardware, every optimizer step hashed, and a **collective verification scheme where independent volunteers each certify individual steps**. 1.61B params, 400B tokens, 80,957 steps. This is the closest thing to "readable/verifiable model, community checks it."
- **Community-trained + fully open, but capability-first:** **Prime Intellect INTELLECT-1/2** (community-donated compute, open weights) and **Ai2 OLMo** (fully open, not crowd compute). These are the realistic templates for "a community trains an open model," even though interpretability isn't the pitch.
- **Readability of tiny models** is a separate, mostly individual effort (TinyStories/nanoGPT replications, "LLMs-from-scratch," "glass-box"/readable-net research). Community *size* is huge but there is no governance/release infrastructure around it.

### Data-license hygiene (notable)
- **Permissive / redistributable:** The Pile (MIT), Dolma + FineWeb(-Edu) + AmberDatasets (ODC-BY), RedPajama (Apache-2.0), The Stack (permissive-code filtered, opt-out).
- **Restricted weights:** BLOOM (**BLOOM-RAIL**, use-based restrictions), OPT (**non-commercial**), Phi-2/3 (**non-commercial**). These are the classic "open weights ≠ open" cases.
- **Mixed:** OLMo/Dolma combines **ODC-BY** data with **AI2 ImpACT**-licensed artifacts — read per-artifact.
- **Off-spec:** TinyStories data is **CDLA-Sharing-1.0** (a data-sharing license, not a software licence; synthetic content), which trips people expecting MIT/Apache.

## Corrections to the brief
- **MiniLLM is NOT a Meta project.** "MiniLLM: On-Policy Distillation of Large Language Models" (arXiv:2306.08543) is by Yuxian Gu & Minlie Huang (Tsinghua) + Li Dong & Furu Wei (Microsoft Research); code lives at `github.com/microsoft/LMOps/tree/main/minillm`. It's a distillation method (120M–13B students), not an open training community.
- **Petals is not a pretraining project** — it is collaborative *inference/fine-tuning* of already-trained big models.
